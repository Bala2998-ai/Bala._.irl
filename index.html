01 Origin

I did not start in product. I started in writers’ rooms, learning why people do what they do. That turned out to be the advantage.

Chapter 01 of 08

It started in a writers’ room, not a marketing team.

Assisting on scripts is not a writing job. It is a psychology job. A scene only works if the audience believes a person would truly do that, in that moment, for that reason, so you spend your days interrogating motive. What does this character want? What are they afraid of? What would make them change their mind, and what would make them walk out? That is customer research before anyone calls it customer research. Three years of it taught me to hear the thing underneath what someone says: the objection behind the question, the fear behind the delay, the story a person is already telling themselves before you arrive. Everything since has been the same job at a higher altitude.
